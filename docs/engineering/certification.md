They are not GxP, CSV, or regulatory validation.

Platform Component CERTIFIED has the same technical-only meaning. See
[Component lifecycle](../platform-components/certification.md).

Official MQTT Temperature, REST Equipment, and OEE templates are
CERTIFIED Golden Paths. OEE commercial availability is FUTURE. Generated
product instances start as DEVELOPMENT and remain independently certified
by platform process, not by this documentation.
