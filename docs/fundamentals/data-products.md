# What is Pharma Data Factory?

Owner: Platform Team  
Last reviewed: 2026-08-19  
Audience: PLATFORM USER  
Version: MVP 1.1

Pharma Data Factory is the **engineering and governance Control Plane**
for manufacturing Data Products. Backstage is the framework, not the
product identity.

It does not replace ERP, MES, LIMS, EWM, Historian, CMO platforms, or
other IT/OT systems of record.

## Why Data Products?

Core systems stay standard so operations, validation, and vendor support
remain predictable. Innovation happens in independently evolving Data
Products that expose governed interfaces to consumers.

Data Products are digital capabilities with:

- a versioned Data Contract
- quality checks
- compatibility rules
- CI/CD
- TechDocs
- Catalog registration

They are not copies of source-system databases and not a second ERP.

## Keep Core Systems Standard

Keep core systems standard. Innovate through Data Products.
